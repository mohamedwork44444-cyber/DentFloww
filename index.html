Features

Everything your clinic needs

Powerful tools designed specifically for dental practices — from solo practitioners to multi-location clinics.

📅

Appointment Management

Drag-and-drop scheduling, automated reminders via SMS and email, and smart conflict detection to prevent double-bookings.

📋

Patient Records

Comprehensive digital records with treatment history, X-ray attachments, clinical notes, and HIPAA-compliant data storage.

🤖

AI Appointment Assistant

Our AI handles rescheduling requests, answers patient questions 24/7, and predicts no-shows with 92% accuracy.

🌐

Online Booking

A beautiful, customizable booking page that integrates with your website. Patients can self-schedule in under 60 seconds.

Analytics Dashboard

Real-time metrics on revenue, patient retention, treatment acceptance rates, and operational efficiency — all in one place.

Team Management

Manage hygienists, assistants, and front-desk staff with role-based permissions, shift scheduling, and performance tracking.
